How it's handled

Every complaint moves through three stages.

  1. SubmittedYou file the complaint here or at the assembly office. It gets a reference number instantly.
  2. ReviewedA staff member assigns it to the right department and marks it "In progress."
  3. ResolvedThe issue is fixed on the ground and the record is closed, with a note on what was done.
